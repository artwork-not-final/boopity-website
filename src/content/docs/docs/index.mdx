## Run your business

- [Services & rates](/docs/guides/services/) — set prices, extra-pet rates, and portal availability.
- [Clients & pets](/docs/guides/clients-and-pets/) — save contact details, emergency contacts, and care instructions.
- [Manage bookings](/docs/guides/bookings/) — approve requests, record visits, and handle cancellations.
- [Booking hours & rules](/docs/guides/booking-rules/) — choose hours, notice periods, and days off.
- [Invite your clients](/docs/guides/client-portal/) — share access and explain their first sign-in.
- [Payments & refunds](/docs/guides/payments/) — record money received, reduce charges, and correct mistakes.

## Change your settings

- [Business & account settings](/docs/guides/business-and-account/) — change branding, time zone, currency, or your email.
- [Connect your email service](/docs/guides/email/) — choose a provider or enter SMTP settings.
- [Set up Resend](/docs/guides/resend/) — add your domain, copy the DNS records, and test your first code.
- [Add Google sign-in](/docs/guides/google-sign-in/) — create the connection and choose the right account.
- [Set up Stripe payments](/docs/guides/stripe/) — connect your account, try a practice payment and refund, then go live.
