Start with a service, a client, and their pet. This example uses a $25 dog walk for Alex and their dog, Scout; use your own details when following along.

## Add a service

In **Services & rates → New service**, enter Dog walking, 30 minutes, and $25. Check the price preview and save.

New services are available to you but hidden from clients until you offer them in the portal. [Service and pricing help](/docs/guides/services/) covers extra pets and all-day care.

## Add your client and their pet

In **Clients & pets → New client**, add Alex’s name and email and select **Save client**. Open their **Pets** tab and choose **Add pet** to add Scout. Save, then open the pet to add care notes. You can invite Alex to sign in later.

## Set your booking hours

In **Portal & rules**, select a weekday and enter your opening and closing times, then select **Save changes**. These determine available times for both you and your clients. There are no hours until you add them.

For exceptions, you can select **Book outside opening hours** or **Waive minimum booking notice** when booking. Clients cannot use these options, and overlapping bookings are still blocked.

## Create the booking

Select **Bookings → New booking**. Choose Alex, Dog walking, Scout, a date, and a time. Review the total and select **Confirm booking**. You should see the confirmed visit in your calendar. [Booking help](/docs/guides/bookings/) covers requests, past visits, and cancellations.

## Record a payment

Once Alex has paid, open the booking’s **Payments** tab. Choose **Record payment**, enter the amount actually received, select **Cash** if that is how they paid, and save. The remaining balance should decrease by that amount.

This logs money received; it does not charge the client. Don’t add a payment before you’ve received it.

The main **Payments** page lists payments across all bookings. [Payment help](/docs/guides/payments/) covers partial payments, refunds, and corrections.
