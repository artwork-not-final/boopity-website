This walkthrough starts after setup is complete. We’ll use a dog walk for a fictional client, Alex, and their dog, Scout. Your prices and business details can be different.

**Screens shown:** Boopity 1.0.1. No real client information is used.

## 1. Add a service

Open **Services & rates** and select **New service**. Enter a name, duration, and price—for example, Dog walking, 30 minutes, and $25. Check the price preview, then save.

New services aren’t offered in the client portal until you choose to make them available there. You can still use them for bookings you create yourself.

## 2. Add your client and their pet

Open **Clients & pets**, add your client’s name and email, and save. Open that client’s **Pets** tab and add their pet. Include any care instructions you’ll need on a visit.

Adding a client is not the same as inviting them to sign in. You can keep records and make bookings before enabling portal access.

<GuideImage
  src={clients}

## 3. Set your booking hours

Open **Portal & rules**, choose your usual hours, and save. The booking form uses these hours to find available times, even when you create the booking yourself.

For a one-off exception, the sitter form offers **Book outside opening hours** and **Waive minimum booking notice**. These don’t change your normal rules or allow double-booking. Clients cannot use these overrides.

## 4. Create the booking

Return to **Bookings** and select **New booking**. Choose your client, pet, service, date, and time. Review the total and save the booking. Its status tells you whether it is requested, confirmed, or completed.

<GuideImage
  src={bookings}
  alt="Weekly calendar with three confirmed bookings for the fictional business."
  caption="Use Week, Month, or List to see your bookings. Open a booking for its details."
/>

## 5. Record a payment

Open the booking and its **Payments** tab. Record a manual payment with the amount and method, such as $25 in cash. This records money received; it does not charge the client.

You can also review recorded payments from the main **Payments** page. Connecting online checkout is optional and uses your own Stripe account.

## Ready to invite your client?

Follow [Set up the client portal](/docs/guides/client-portal/) before sending an invitation. That guide covers service visibility, booking hours, and cancellation rules.
