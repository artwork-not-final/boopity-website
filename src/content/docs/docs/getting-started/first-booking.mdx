After setup, create a booking in five steps. This example uses a $25 dog walk for Alex and their dog, Scout.

## 1. Add a service

In **Services & rates → New service**, enter Dog walking, 30 minutes, and $25. Check the price preview and save.

New services are available to you but hidden from clients until you offer them in the portal.

<GuideImage
  src={services}
  alt="The Services and rates page showing Dog walking, Drop-in visit, and Pet sitting with their prices."
  caption="Services & rates"
/>

## 2. Add your client and their pet

In **Clients & pets**, add Alex’s name and email. Open their **Pets** tab to add Scout and any care notes. You can invite Alex to sign in later.

<GuideImage
  src={clients}
  alt="Clients and pets with three fictional clients and their pets."
  caption="Clients & pets"
/>

## 3. Set your booking hours

In **Portal & rules**, save your usual hours. These determine available times for both you and your clients.

For exceptions, you can select **Book outside opening hours** or **Waive minimum booking notice** when booking. Clients cannot use these options, and overlapping bookings are still blocked.

## 4. Create the booking

Select **Bookings → New booking**. Choose Alex, Scout, Dog walking, a date, and a time. Review the total and save.

<GuideImage
  src={bookings}
  alt="Weekly calendar with three confirmed bookings for the fictional business."
  caption="Bookings · Week view"
/>

## 5. Record a payment

In the booking’s **Payments** tab, record $25 in cash. This logs money received; it does not charge the client.

The main **Payments** page lists payments across all bookings.
