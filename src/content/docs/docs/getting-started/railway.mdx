Railway runs Boopity for you. You won’t need Terminal, Docker on your computer, or a separate database.

## Before you start

- **Hosting:** Railway’s Hobby plan starts at **$5 per month**, including $5 of resource usage. That is a minimum, not a spending cap. Check [current pricing](https://railway.com/pricing) before deploying.
- **Email:** Have a Resend or SMTP account and an approved sender address ready. Boopity uses this service to send sign-in codes. [Connect your email service](/docs/guides/email/) explains how to prepare either option.

Boopity is free. Hosting, email, domains, and payment-processing fees are separate. Boopity may receive referral credit or commission if you sign up through the link below or deploy through this template.

## Create a Railway account

New to Railway? [Create your Railway account](https://railway.com?referralCode=oH-G_8), then return here to deploy Boopity. Already have an account? Continue below.

## Deploy Boopity

<LinkButton href="https://railway.com/new/template/boopity" icon="external">
  Deploy on Railway
</LinkButton>

Sign in to Railway if needed. Choose **New Project** and click **Deploy**. The Boopity service should already say **Ready to be deployed**; leave its configuration unchanged. If Railway asks you to choose a plan or add billing details, review the cost before continuing.

<GuideImage
  src={deploy}
  alt="Railway’s Deploy Boopity screen with New Project selected, the service ready to be deployed, and a Deploy button."
  caption="No configuration values to enter."
/>

Wait for deployment to finish, then click the **Boopity** service. **Deployment successful** means your site is running. Railway supplies its own HTTPS address; you can add a custom domain later.

## Open your private setup link

In the service’s **Deployments** tab, select **View logs**. Find **Finish setup** and open the link beside it. If it isn’t clickable, copy the complete link into your browser’s address bar. On the welcome page, choose **Start setup**.

<GuideImage
  src={service}
  alt="The Boopity service on Railway showing Deployment successful and the View logs button."
  caption="View logs contains your private Finish setup link."
/>

:::caution[Keep this link private]
It gives access to setup. Don’t share it or a screenshot of the logs. The ordinary website address won’t unlock a new installation.
:::

If the link expires **before you start**, use the deployment’s three-dot menu to **Restart**, then open the new link in its logs. If you’ve already started, return in the same browser. [Help getting back into setup](/docs/help/troubleshooting/#i-cant-get-back-into-setup).

## Your account

Enter your name and an email address you can check, then select **Save and continue**. This will be your owner account.

## Email delivery

Choose **Resend** or **SMTP**. Enter your approved sender address and the credentials from your email service, then select **Save and continue**. Follow the [email connection instructions](/docs/guides/email/) if you need help with a field. Keep credentials private and enter them only in your own installation.

## Finish the wizard

- **Business:** Add your business name, colors, currency, and time zone.
- **Verify your inbox:** Request a code and enter it to create your owner account. Check spam if it doesn’t arrive; wait for the timer before resending.
- **Google sign-in:** Optional. Skip it for now to use email codes, or [connect Google](/docs/guides/google-sign-in/) with the same email as your owner account.
- **Review & finish:** Check your details and finish setup. You’ll arrive at **Bookings** with a getting-started checklist.

**Next:** [Make your first booking](/docs/getting-started/first-booking/), then [invite a client](/docs/guides/client-portal/).

## Keep your records safe

Your records and private keys live on the included Railway volume. Keep one running instance and don’t delete that volume. **Automatic backups are not included.** Set up [backups](/docs/help/backups-and-updates/) before using Boopity for real clients.

The template uses a fixed release; updates are your choice, not automatic. You look after your Railway account, email service, backups, and updates. See [community support](/about/#support) if you need help.
