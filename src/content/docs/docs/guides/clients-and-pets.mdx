Create a client record for the person arranging care, then add their pets to that record. Creating a record does not send an invitation or give the client access to your site.

## Add a client

1. Open **Clients & pets → New client**.
2. Enter their first and last name, email, and phone number.
3. Add their address and an emergency contact if you have them.
4. Put information meant only for you under **Private client notes**.
5. Select **Save client**.

Use the email the client intends to sign in with. You can leave invitations until you’re ready for online booking.

Find a saved client by typing their name, email, or phone in the search field. Change the status filter if you’re looking for an archived record.

## Add their pets

Open the client’s **Pets** tab and choose **Add pet**. Enter the name and species, then select **Add pet** to save.

Open the pet to add its breed, medication, allergies, medical conditions, feeding instructions, and other care details. Select **Save care details** when you’re done.

## What the client can see

Clients can see their pets’ basic details, such as name, species, breed, and status. They cannot edit these records or see your saved care instructions, medical details, or private notes in the portal.

To share a visit update, use the booking’s **Client-visible visit update**, not a private note. [Invite a client](/docs/guides/client-portal/) when you’re ready for them to sign in.

## Change a client’s email

Edit **Email** in the client’s **Contact** tab and save. This removes their existing portal access. Create a new invitation for the replacement address; changing the contact record alone doesn’t reconnect their account.

## Archive a client or pet

Use **Archive client** or **Archive pet** when you no longer need to book care for them. Their history stays available, but they can’t be selected for new bookings.

Archiving a client also removes their portal access. To welcome them back, reactivate the client and any pets you need, then create a new invitation.
