Each service defines the type of care, its length, and its price. Start with the services you actually offer; you can add more later.

## Add a service

1. Open **Services & rates → New service**.
2. Enter a name clients will recognize, such as **Dog walking** or **Overnight pet sitting**.
3. Set the visit length, or choose **All-day / multi-day care** for a service charged by the day.
4. Enter the price and any additional-pet price. Check the one-pet and two-pet previews.
5. Select **Save service**.

## Price a booking with more than one pet

For a $25 visit with a $10 additional-pet price:

| Pets  | Visit total |
| ----- | ----------- |
| One   | $25         |
| Two   | $35         |
| Three | $45         |

Leaving the additional-pet price at **0** means each pet is charged the full base price. It does **not** mean extra pets are free. In that example, two pets would cost $50. Check the preview before saving.

For all-day care, the first and last dates both count. Monday through Wednesday is three days, so a $40 daily rate for one pet totals $120.

## Let clients book the service

New services are hidden from the client portal. You can still use them when creating bookings yourself.

Open the saved service and select **Offer in the client portal**. This change saves immediately. Clients also need portal access, and you need [booking hours](/docs/guides/booking-rules/) that make the service available.

Clear the option to hide a service from clients without removing it from your own booking form.

## Change a rate or retire a service

Open the service, edit its details, and select **Save service**. Existing bookings keep their saved price; the new rate applies to new bookings.

Choose **Archive service** when you no longer offer it. It disappears from new-booking choices, but old bookings remain. If you reactivate it later, check whether it is offered in the portal before inviting clients to use it.
