title: Manage bookings
description: Create visits, approve client requests, record past work, and handle cancellations.
---

Use **Week** or **Month** to see your schedule, or **List** to find a particular visit. Search by client, pet, or service; search results include other dates, not just the week on screen. The status filter helps you find requests that need attention.

## Book a visit for a client

1. Open **Bookings → New booking**.
2. Choose the client, service, and pets receiving care.
3. Choose a date and an available start time. For all-day care, choose the start date and last day of care instead.
4. Add a **Booking message** if needed. The client can read it.
5. Check the dates, pets, total, and cancellation notice in the summary, then select **Confirm booking**.

Bookings you create are confirmed immediately. Payment is separate: confirming a booking does not charge the client.

No times available? [Check your hours and booking rules](/docs/guides/booking-rules/#why-a-time-isnt-available).

## Make an exception

For a future booking, the sitter can select **Book outside opening hours** or **Waive minimum booking notice**. These options are not available to clients.

They don’t override an unavailable date, the advance-booking window, or an overlapping reservation. Boopity manages one schedule, not separate staff calendars or multiple boarding spaces.

## Record work you’ve already done

Use **New booking** and select a past date and time. Once the whole visit is in the past, the button changes to **Record past booking**. It saves as completed, using the service’s current rate.

Boopity warns about overlaps with other records, but you can still record past work. Check that you aren’t entering the same visit twice. Recording it doesn’t record a payment; add money received or adjust the charge in its **Payments** tab.

## Review a client’s request

If your rules require approval, a client’s booking appears as requested rather than confirmed.

Open it and choose **Approve request** or **Decline request**. Pending requests hold their time slot until your approval deadline or the visit starts, whichever comes first. An expired request no longer reserves the slot.

Check requests in Boopity regularly. It does not automatically email booking notifications to you or the client.

## Add notes and finish a visit

Open the booking’s **Overview** tab:

- **Private sitter notes** are for you.
- **Client-visible visit update** is shared with the client in their portal.

Save your changes. After the visit has ended, choose **Mark completed**. This updates the booking status, not its payment balance.

The **History** tab records booking changes. The **Payments** tab shows money received, refunds, and adjustments for that booking.

## Cancel or move a booking

Open the booking, enter a **Cancellation reason**, and choose **Cancel booking**. The client can see the reason. Sitters can make cancellation exceptions even after the client’s deadline.

To move a visit, cancel the original and create a new booking for the replacement date. There isn’t a reschedule editor. If the original has a payment, review that balance separately; it won’t move to the new booking automatically.

**Cancelling does not refund a payment or waive the charge.** Use the booking’s [payment controls](/docs/guides/payments/#refunds-credits-and-corrections) for either action.

## Review cancellations

The **Cancellations** page collects cancelled bookings. Open one, add a private **Review note** if needed, and select **Mark reviewed** when you’ve dealt with it.

Marking it reviewed is only a record that you’ve checked it. It does not refund money, reduce the charge, or contact the client.
