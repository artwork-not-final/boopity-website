---
title: Invite your clients
description: Let clients view their pets, request bookings, and manage visits on your website.
---

Invite clients to book and manage visits on your website. Each invitation is tied to their email address.

## Before you invite anyone

1. In **Services & rates**, open a service and select **Offer in the client portal**.
2. In **Portal & rules**, [set your opening hours, booking notice, and cancellation notice](/docs/guides/booking-rules/).
3. Choose whether bookings need your approval or are confirmed immediately.
4. Select **Allow invited clients to sign in** and save.

Set opening hours first, or clients won’t see available times.

## Share an invitation

1. Open **Clients & pets**, choose the client, and check the email in **Contact**.
2. Open **Portal access** and select **Create invitation**.
3. Copy the **Private one-time invitation link** before leaving the page. It is shown only once.
4. Send the link yourself by email or a private message. Boopity does not send invitations automatically.

If **Create invitation** is disabled, check that the client is active, has an email address, and that the portal is enabled.

Keep invitation links private. They work once, expire after seven days, and become invalid when replaced.

## What your client does

The client opens the link and signs in with the invited email, using a code or **Continue with Google** if enabled. They then select **Accept & continue** to open **My bookings**.

After that first visit, they can sign in from your ordinary website address. They don’t need the invitation again.

To book, they choose a service, their pets, a date, and an available time. Depending on your rules, the final button is **Send booking request** or **Confirm booking**. The booking summary shows which applies and when they can cancel.

## What clients can do

- See their own pets and bookings.
- Request or book services you offer in the portal.
- Cancel within the notice period saved with their booking.
- Pay online when you have connected Stripe and enabled online payments.

Clients only see their own records. They contact you to change pet details; your care instructions and private notes are not shown in their portal. Booking messages and client-visible visit updates are shared.

## Cancellations and payments

After the cancellation deadline, clients must contact you for an exception.

**Cancellation does not issue a refund.** Arrange refunds separately; marking a cancellation reviewed does not move money.

Online payment is separate from booking. Clients can pay an outstanding balance on a confirmed or completed booking when live Stripe checkout is enabled. [Connect Stripe](/docs/guides/stripe/) before offering this to real clients.

## Change a client’s sign-in email

Save the replacement **Email** in the client’s **Contact** tab. Their previous portal access is removed. Create and privately send a new invitation, and ask them to sign in with the replacement address.

## Removing access

Select **Revoke portal access** in the client’s **Portal access** tab. This also invalidates their outstanding invitations. Archiving a client or changing their email revokes access too; reactivating the client does not restore it.

Turning the portal off signs clients out without affecting your sitter workspace. It is a pause, not a permanent revocation: unexpired invitations may work when you turn it back on. Revoke a client’s access individually if you don’t want them to return.
